The record

Tianjin gave the foundations and the habit of service: two years of rowing, a student-rights role, a hundred freshmen guided in, essays on Darboux and Hilbert. Then came a year in Hong Kong, a first office and a first microphone in Chengdu, and a mock UN conference in Geneva.

Why it matters

The pieces were there before the PhD: random walks already in the undergraduate thesis, and a habit of speaking up for other students that became the rep work of Chapter 06.

2021–25 in the School of Mathematics, the final year at Bristol via the 3+1 route. Every walker on this site starts here.

Entered the School of Mathematics at Tianjin University in the 2021 cohort, in an honours mathematics class, reading Mathematics and Applied Mathematics until June 2025 — analysis, advanced algebra, real and complex analysis, mathematical modelling — and qualifying for exemption from the national postgraduate entrance exam.

The 3+1 route: the first shortcut

Nov 2022 – Sep 2025 Completed

The degree’s last stage ran through the 3+1 route between Tianjin University and the University of Bristol: three years at Tianjin (the third on exchange in Hong Kong), then a one-year MSc at Bristol. Selection came in November 2022; the move was made in September 2024. Because the bachelor’s (to June 2025) and the master’s (from September 2024) overlap, the plate draws this as a shortcut — a long-range jump that skips a year.

An exchange year at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)

Sep 2023 – Jun 2024 Completed

Spent the 2023–24 academic year as an exchange student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, taking partial differential equations, numerical solution of PDEs and applied statistics. It was where the mathematics turned computational — and where a first neural-network research project began.

MSc Engineering Mathematics, University of Bristol, with Distinction (the top UK grade)

Sep 2024 – Sep 2025 Completed

Moved to Bristol in September 2024 for the MSc in Engineering Mathematics. The modules ran from statistical computing and empirical methods, information theory and coding, and renewable energy to mathematical modelling in biology, medicine and public health, methods of AI, and skills in mathematical research. It finished with a Distinction and a dissertation on first passage — see Chapter 01.
