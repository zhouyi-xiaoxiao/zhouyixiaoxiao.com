The record

Some of the record keeps itself: nearly four thousand videos on a phone since 2023, a LinkedIn headline frozen in 2024, a first personal website. This chapter is the signal — what was published, what was kept, and how it is being turned into something others can watch.

Two of the studio’s videos are out on WeChat Channels under the name 周易潇潇 (Zhouyi Xiaoxiao): the Festival of Enterprise pitch (June 2026) and the fellowship interview (August 2026). Both belong to the venture in Chapter 07, which keeps the rest of what was published about it: the pitch deck, the Careers Service spotlight and the enterprise blog. The research talk ‘Hidden Routes in First-Passage Time’, a bilingual web deck, is in Chapter 01.

And a making-of: a first prototype of this site in August, a blunt critique a month later — not enough light, achievements hard to find — and the plate you saw on the front page as the answer.

Why it matters

Work that no one sees has not quite arrived: posts, talks and videos, in two languages, are how it reaches people.

Studio

A WeChat content studio

Launched August 2026: an agent-run studio turning years of phone footage into short videos for WeChat — a human always presses publish.

It publishes to WeChat Channels (视频号, WeChat’s short-video feed) and a WeChat Official Account (公众号, its long-form channel). Four goals frame it: map every video in the archive; build a voice and a digital presence with clear AI disclosure; profile the speaking style and suggest how to improve it; and semi-automate publishing. A resumable pass scanned 3,943 of the 3,947 videos and found speech in 1,052 — and only such counts are ever published. The channel’s name is 周易潇潇.

This site: First Passage, Edition 01

28 Sep 2026 Edition 01 · live

The site you are reading, in its first edition: a live first-passage experiment in which walkers leave Tianjin and search a luminous plate of 2021 to now for whatever you choose to measure. It replaces the page that, since April 2026, simply forwarded zhouyixiaoxiao.com to LinkedIn. It was designed and built with a large team of AI agents working to one specification, with every fact drawn from a verified, privacy-tagged record — and nothing private shipped.

A camera roll as a diary: 3,947 videos

Sep 2023 – Aug 2026 Completed

An inventory of the personal camera roll in August 2026 counted 3,947 videos shot between 3 September 2023 and 27 August 2026 — 140 in 2023, 1,310 in 2024, 1,574 in 2025 and 923 in 2026 up to August. Month by month, the counts trace the seasons of a life: quiet stretches, and surges whenever a new city or a new project began. Only these aggregates are published; the clips themselves stay private. They are the raw material for the WeChat studio.
