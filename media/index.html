Media, posts and video

What I have published: WeChat videos and referenced articles, LinkedIn as a notebook, bilingual subtitles, this site.

Some of the record keeps itself: nearly four thousand videos on a phone since 2023, two years of milestone posts on LinkedIn, a first personal website. This chapter is the signal — what was published, what was kept, and how it is being turned into something worth watching.

In October and November 2026 the WeChat Channels account 周易潇潇 (Zhouyi Xiaoxiao) put out four to-camera videos, with long-form articles on the Official Account of the same name; one of them, on whether a one-year master’s abroad is still worth the money in the age of AI, cites 76 references. Two earlier videos on the channel, the Festival of Enterprise pitch (June 2026) and the fellowship interview (August 2026), belong to the venture in Chapter 07, which keeps the rest of what was published about it: the Careers Service spotlight and the enterprise blog. The research talk ‘Hidden Routes in First-Passage Time’, a bilingual web deck, is in Chapter 01.

And a making-of: a first prototype of this site in August, a blunt critique a month later — not enough light, achievements hard to find — and the plate you saw on the front page as the answer.

A WeChat content studio

It publishes to WeChat Channels (WeChat’s short-video feed) and a WeChat Official Account (its long-form channel). In October and November 2026 they carried four to-camera videos and long-form articles: figures are checked before they go in, and the sources are listed at the end. A scan of the archive that could pause and pick up again covered 3,943 of the 3,947 videos and found speech in 1,052 — and only such counts are ever published. The channel’s name is 周易潇潇.

10 Jun – 27 Aug 2026 · Shipped

A home-built bilingual subtitle pipeline

The local build of ffmpeg shipped without a subtitle renderer, so subtitles took another route: every bilingual cue is rendered as a transparent image and overlaid, in chunks of about ninety seconds processed in parallel, so an 8 min 44 s 4K clip renders in about two and a half minutes. A native Swift tool followed in July, and caption rules in August: at most two lines, 18 Chinese or 46 English characters per line, zero overlaps, and a terminology audit.

Sep 2023 – Aug 2026 · Completed

A camera roll as a diary: 3,947 videos

An inventory of the personal camera roll in August 2026 counted 3,947 videos shot between 3 September 2023 and 27 August 2026 — 140 in 2023, 1,310 in 2024, 1,574 in 2025 and 923 in 2026 up to August. Month by month, the counts show quiet stretches and clear peaks. Only these aggregates are published; the clips themselves stay private. They are the raw material for the WeChat studio.

28 Sep 2026 · Live

This site: So far.

The site you are reading draws the record as a first-passage process, the mathematics of the PhD: random walkers leave Tianjin and stop when they first reach an item, on a timeline from 2021 to now, that matches what you search for. It replaces the page that, since April 2026, simply forwarded zhouyixiaoxiao.com to LinkedIn. It was designed to one written specification and built with AI coding agents; every fact comes from a verified, privacy-tagged record, and nothing private shipped.
