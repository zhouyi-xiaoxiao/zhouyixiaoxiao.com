Side projects

What I built in the gaps, mostly so someone else could move faster: a maths guide, a travel-story app, a cursor tool, an agent bridge.

The account dates from December 2021, but the building is recent: almost all of it lands in 2026 — two hackathons in London, a route-decision page for a snowy Iceland drive, two guides for Bristol’s Master of Laws students. Outside my own repositories, I contribute to Inspect, the UK AI Security Institute’s open-source framework for evaluating AI: by 25 Nov 2026, 14 of my pull requests had been merged across 7 repositories of its ecosystem, and 3 security reports I filed had been fixed, each credited by name.

And the small fixes in between: a browser that went deaf, seven dead ends before a working keyboard shortcut, an always-on agent host that taught its lessons and was then deliberately uninstalled.

19–28 Apr 2026 · Live demo

London Cuts

A creator tool for a single-place trip: photos placed by their own metadata, written stories and AI-generated postcards in six art styles, with public reader pages in three visual modes (Punk, Fashion, Cinema), a map atlas and PDF or PNG export. The live demo tells ‘A Year Around London’ in thirteen stops and ‘A Week in Reykjavík’, and it has entry points for AI agents too: an llms.txt, an OpenAPI description and an MCP endpoint (MCP is an open protocol that lets AI call outside tools). London Cuts is a working name.

5–9 Mar 2026 · Completed

SocialOS: a local-only social-memory assistant, built in five days

SocialOS turns raw notes, voice memos, screenshots and encounters into structured memory about people and events, reflections on yourself and drafts for each platform, delivered through a publishing queue that a human approves. It runs only on the local machine. It was built for the UK AI Agent Hack in five days, with 1,222 commits between 5 and 9 March 2026 — 1,084 of them tagged ‘[autodev]’: commits made by the automated coding agents set up for that week. The public repository keeps the pitch pack and the evidence.

22 Jul 2023 · Live

The Tianjin University maths guide

Rather than just taking the courses, I wrote a guide to them for the students who come after. Started in July 2023, the Tianjin University maths undergraduate guide gathers what a student needs — a foreword, the core courses, the degree programmes and how to contribute — first as a quick guide with a document library, then as a MkDocs site. It has been served at zhouyixiaoxiao.info since December 2025 and is the oldest thing on this page, still online.

zhouyixiaoxiao.info (external link)

29–30 May 2026 · Shipped

CatchMouse, rebuilt for Apple Silicon

A clean Swift rewrite of CatchMouse, an Intel-only utility from 2011: a menu-bar agent that warps the cursor to the left, centre or right display with global hotkeys, cycles between displays, remembers custom hotkeys per monitor, flashes display numbers and offers command-line verbs. It builds a universal app with only the command-line tools and needs no accessibility permission. MIT-licensed.

27–29 Jun 2026 · In daily use

Pro Agent Bridge

A local MCP server (MCP is an open protocol that lets AI call outside tools) that turns ChatGPT’s developer mode into a bounded coding and hand-off surface for one repository: reading files, searching, exact edits, git inspection and safe verification commands. It extends the MIT-licensed open-source CodexPro by rebel0789 with hand-off from ChatGPT Pro to Claude Code, an AI coding agent, saved-session capture and hardened polling, and it is still in daily use. Credit where it is due: the foundation is upstream.
