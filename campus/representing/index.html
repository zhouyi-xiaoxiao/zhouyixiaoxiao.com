The record

Some roles are about carrying other people’s voices — and one, for seven minutes, was about using his own.

Representation here is practical: an anonymous feedback form that reached every PhD student in the school, points carried up through school and faculty forums, and a bowling night that put academics and PhD students on the same lanes.

And the other side of the table: reading applications for the Spärck AI Scholarships, answering families’ questions at open days, running a double pendulum for children at a science festival — and, on 3 June 2026, seven minutes on stage explaining what a maths PhD is actually like.

Why it matters

Most of what makes a PhD harder is small and fixable — once someone collects it and carries it to the room where decisions are made.

A month into the PhD: speaking for PhD students in SEMT (Engineering Mathematics and Technology), 2025–26. Rep certificate, July 2026.

Stepped forward on 1 December 2025; after the Students’ Union’s rep and communication training, the role was confirmed in January 2026. Through the year’s school and faculty rep forums, PhD students’ points were carried upward — in person, through the co-rep or online — and the careers service agreed to add a general careers Q&A to a PhD lunch-and-learn.

Seven minutes of stand-up about a maths PhD

12 Feb – 3 Jun 2026 Performed

‘The Lighter Side of Research’, run by the doctoral colleges of Bath, Bristol and Bath Spa, trains PhD researchers to turn their research into stand-up. After three workshops in Bath and Bristol through the spring, the programme’s first public show took place on 3 June 2026 at The Mount Without in Bristol, and the set — about seven minutes on doing a maths PhD — ran in the first half. The billing read: Engineering Mathematics, Bristol. Random walks, networks, and the strange peaks and valleys hidden in motion.

Reading panel, Spärck AI Scholarships

11 May – 10 Jun 2026 Completed

Served on a twelve-member Faculty of Science and Engineering panel reviewing applications for the Spärck AI Scholarships at Bristol in May and June 2026: after a training session, each application was scored against a shared marking scheme, and results went to applicants on 10 June before interviews. It was a view of AI talent from the selection side.

From a seven-response survey to eighty seats

4–17 Jun 2026 Completed

At the School PhD director’s request, drafted and circulated a short interest check on structured writing retreats for SEMT PhD students. Every one of the seven respondents said yes; the director confirmed two faculty retreat sessions of forty places each, and they were advertised to PhD students for July 2026. A tiny data story with a tangible result.
