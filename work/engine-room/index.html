Why a cloud-communications company? A month before joining, an argument had taken shape in a series of memos: AI agents will reach the real world through messaging, because an agent that can find people where they already are is one that gets things done. Since June the work has tested that from the inside — the plumbing first, then products for people.

Why it matters

The aim: move the company from trying AI to using it every day — safely, with governance built in, and with a person confirming before anything is sent, shared or approved.

R&D Scientist (AI), Yuexin International

Three strands: the AI platform and model gateway, AI tools for colleagues, and AI for global messaging.

Joined in June 2026, alongside the PhD, at a company whose business spans messaging, voice and AI. Of the three strands, the widest is habit rather than software: AI training for every department, and the scattered pilots gathered into one programme in July. The entries below are what got built.

An internal AI gateway with single sign-on

10–13 Jun 2026 Live

Within four days an open-source gateway for large language models (LLMs) became the company’s internal AI platform, the Yuexin Intelligent Open Platform. The gateway was chosen by reading the source code of two candidates rather than their brochures. The work covered a deployment runbook, white-labelling and full Chinese localisation of the admin interface, a mobile layout, and a self-service portal where colleagues sign in with Feishu (also known as Lark, a workplace chat-and-docs suite), get a key and see their own usage. Router retries, fallbacks and per-member budgets followed, and a pre-launch audit of model and permission boundaries closed the week.

An enterprise platform for governed AI access

25–31 Jul 2026 Live

The company’s enterprise AI unified-access and governance platform went from local build to live in a week, launching on 31 July 2026. It is a self-built portal over an open-source gateway: organisations, teams and virtual keys, model allow-lists, budgets and usage, a data-loss-prevention hook and an audit hash-chain, with the gateway’s own admin interface closed to the internet. Around launch it went through several rounds of adversarial review by parallel AI agents; privacy-canary checks that planted synthetic identifiers and confirmed none survived in storage or logs; and formal production acceptance, with a receipt for every model.

Yuexin AI Assistant — web and Windows

21 Sep 2026 Live

In one day, an assistant for company staff was built and launched on the company’s model gateway. Colleagues sign in with Feishu and are provisioned automatically, with a personal monthly quota and self-serve API keys. The web app streams its answers, folds away the model’s reasoning, renders Markdown and maths, accepts images and has light and dark themes; a Windows desktop client shipped alongside it, with a download page. Before release it went through three parallel security reviews and an independent review by a second model.
